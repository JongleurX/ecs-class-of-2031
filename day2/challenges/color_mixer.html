Teacher note: The script currently names six specific blends (gray, purple, light blue, green, pink, orange). Other matrix entries here use common classroom-friendly names to support discussion and extension ideas.

Print setting suggestion: Portrait, "Actual size" (100%), and avoid "Fit to page" for best table proportions on US Letter (8.5 x 11 in).
